The life of a booking
  1. The client books. They pick a package, a sunrise or sunset time, and pay the deposit (card or Zelle).
  2. You both get an email. They get a confirmation; you get a “new booking” notice.
  3. They tell you about the shoot. Their confirmation links to a short form: location, the look, who or what you’re shooting. The booking moves to Prepped.
  4. Reminders go out on their own, 3 days and 1 day before the session.
  5. You shoot, then edit. Tap Mark shot, then Start editing.
  6. You send the photos. Tap Photos, add them, and tap Send. The client gets a link to their gallery.
  7. Watermarked until paid. The client sees proofs with your watermark until the balance is paid. Then the clean, full-size photos unlock to download.
  8. A week later, they get a short note asking how the photos turned out.

Offers: a custom package for one client
  1. Go to Offers and tap Make an offer.
  2. Fill in the title, a line or two about the shoot, the price and the deposit. Tap Save.
  3. Tap Send link and send it in your DM or text.
  4. The client opens it, taps Pick a time and books it like any package.

Offers never show on your site. The link keeps working until you untick Open for booking on that offer, so it can be booked more than once until then.

Getting paid
Deposit by card
Paid on Stripe. The booking confirms itself.
Deposit by Zelle
The time is held while it arrives. When the client taps “I’ve sent it”, you get an email. Check your bank, then tap Confirm in that email or received on the booking.
The balance
Paid online in their gallery, by Zelle, or in person. If they pay you in person, tap Mark paid on the booking and their photos unlock.
Mistakes and Undo

Most taps happen right away and show an Undo button for a few seconds. Anything that emails the client (sending a gallery, cancelling, marking paid, confirming Zelle) waits about 10 seconds first, so Undo can stop it. Once the email has gone, it can’t be undone.

Good to know
  • No double bookings. Two people can never get the same time.
  • Holds: a card payment holds the time for 30 minutes. A Zelle deposit holds it for the hours set under Setup › Payments & email.
  • Shoots per week counts held times as well as paid ones.
  • Needs attention: someone paid after their hold ran out and the time had gone to someone else. Refund their deposit and offer them another date.
  • Client links are private. Each client’s booking and gallery pages work only from the link in their emails.
